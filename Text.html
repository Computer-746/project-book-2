Merry Marbling is an Completed manhwa written and illustrated by Pengki, first released in 2022. The story follows Ha Ian, a high school student known as the "Angel of Christmas" due to his kindness and good reputation. When Baek Yugun, a frail and sickly child, returns to school, Ian offers to help him, but their relationship becomes complicated when it is revealed that Ian's kindness is part of a calculated plan to maintain his image.

The series has been translated in English by Lezhin starting in 2023. There is also an official Chinese translation.
